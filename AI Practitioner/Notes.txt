Automated Metrics to evaluate FM :-
1)ROGUE :- Recall oriented understudy for gisting evaluation
-Evaluates automatic summarization and machine translation systems

-ROUGUE-N :-measure the number of matching N-grams(1 gram = 1 word) between reference and generated text
-So basically matches number of words from reference text (benchmark answer you expect)
and generated text

ROUGE-L :-longest common subsequence between reference and generated text

2)BLEU : Billingual Evaluation Understudy
-Evaluate the quality of generated text , especially translations
-Considers both precision and penalizes too much brevity
-Looks at combination of N-grams (1,2,3,4)

3)BERTscore :-
-EValuates based on semantic similarity between generated texts
-Semantic similarity means compares actual meaning of the generated text
-Uses pretrained BERT models (Bidirectional Encoder Representations from Transformers)
to compare the contextualized embeddings of both texts and computes the cosine similarity between them.

4)Perplexity :- How well the model predicts the next token (lower is better)

Prompt Performance Optimization :-
In Amazon Bedrock, Top‑K and Top‑P are text generation sampling parameters that control how the model chooses the next token during generation. They directly affect creativity, randomness, and determinism of the output.

1)System prompts :- set how the model should behave and repsonse
2)Temperature (0 - 1) :- Creativity of the models output
-Low(eg.0.2) :- outputs are more conservative , focused on most likely response
-High(eg.1.0) :- Outputs are more diverse , creative and unpredicatable

3)Top P (0 to 1) :- considers most likely words as a distribution
-Low(eg.0.2) :- Most likely response
-High(eg.1.0) :- Consider a broad range of possible words , more creative

4) Top K :- Limits the number of probable words
Low(eg.10) :- Youll get top 10 most probable words , more coherent(Coherent means clear, logical, and easy to understand, with all parts fitting together in a sensible way.) reponse
High(eg.500) :- more probable words , more diverse and creative

Length :- set maximum length of answer

Stop sequences :-   Tokens that signal the model to stop generating output

Prompt Latency :- Latency means how fast the model responds 
Its impacted by a few params :-
1)The model size
2)THe model type itself
3)Number of tokens in input

Latency is not impacted by top P , top K or tempaerature

Prompt engineering techniques :-
1)Zero shot prompting :- Present a task to the model without providing examples or explicit 
training for that specific task
-Here you fully rely on models general knowledge.
-The larger and capable the model , more better the response is

2)Few shots prompting :-
-Provide examples of task to the model
-We provide a few shots to the model to perform the task
-If you provide only 1 example , its one shot or single shot

3)CHain of though prompting :-
-Divide a task into sequence of reasoning steps , leading to more structure and coherence
-Using a sentence "use step by step"
-Can be combined with zero shot or single shot prompting

4)Retrieval Augmented Generation RAG:-
-COmbine models capability with external sources of data
-THe initial prompt is then augmented with external information
